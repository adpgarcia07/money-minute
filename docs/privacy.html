Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

The app "money-minute-poster" is a private tool used by the owner of the Money Minute Facebook Page to publish educational video content (Reels) to that Page.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from Page visitors, followers or any other users. It only uses an access token belonging to the Page owner in order to publish content to the owner's own Page.

Third parties

Content is published through the Meta (Facebook) Graph API. Meta's own privacy policy applies to data handled on Facebook.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no user data, there is nothing to delete. See data deletion instructions.
